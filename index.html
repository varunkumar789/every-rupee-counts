Manual backup

Your records are saved locally on this device/browser. Clearing Safari website data, deleting the app or changing phones may erase them. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe. This version does not encrypt backup files.

For privacy, avoid sharing your backup. Files saved to iCloud Drive are no longer strictly local-only.

About this app

No login, analytics, cloud API or automatic bank access. Money is stored as integer paise for exact arithmetic. This is a personal bookkeeping tool, not a bank statement. It cannot identify omitted transactions without reconciliation.
